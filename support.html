Support

We usually reply within 2 business days.

Contact us

Email support@mrkrphl.com with your device model, iOS version, and a short description of the issue. Screenshots help.

For account deletion, you can also delete your account directly in the app under Settings > Account > Delete account.

Common questions

How do I pair with my partner?

During onboarding or from the partner flow, one person generates a 6-character code. The other enters it. Codes expire after 24 hours. Only one active partner is supported at a time.

What can my partner see?

Only tasks you mark as shared. Private tasks stay private. Your partner sees shared task titles, descriptions, due dates, and completion status. They cannot edit your tasks.

How do I unpair?

Open Settings > Your tether > Unpair. Shared tasks on your side revert to private immediately. Either partner can unpair without the other's confirmation.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings > Account > Delete account, confirm by typing DELETE, and submit. This permanently removes your account and data. It cannot be undone.

Does Tether track my screen time?

No. Screen time in Tether is a voluntary daily check-in (Light, Normal, or A lot). We do not access iOS Screen Time APIs or collect per-app usage.

Sign-in issues

Tether uses Google sign-in. Make sure you are on the latest app version. If sign-in fails, try again on a stable network or email us with the error message you see.
