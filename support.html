Common questions

How do I pair with someone?

One of you generates a 6-character code. The other enters it. Codes expire after 24 hours. Twodo is for two people — only one active pair at a time.

What can they see?

Only tasks you share. Private tasks stay yours. On a shared task they can see the title, description, due date, and whether it is done. They cannot edit your tasks. What they shared with you lives on Partner.

How do I unpair?

Either of you can unpair at any time, without the other person's confirmation. Shared tasks on your side go back to private immediately.

How do I delete my account?

Open Me > Account > Delete account, confirm by typing DELETE, and submit. This permanently removes your account and data. It cannot be undone.

Sign-in issues

Twodo uses Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In. Make sure you are on the latest app version. If sign-in fails, try again on a stable network or email us with the error message you see.
