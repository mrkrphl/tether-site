Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 7, 2026

Tether ("we", "us", or "our") is a mobile accountability app for two people who choose to share tasks and check in with each other. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Who we are

Tether is operated by the developer of the iOS app with bundle identifier app.mrkrphl.tether. For privacy questions or account requests, contact us at mrkrphl0705@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you create an account and use Tether, we process:

  • Account information: email address and name from Google sign-in.
  • Profile information: display name, partner color, timezone, and onboarding state.
  • Tasks: titles, descriptions, due dates, recurrence settings, completion status, and whether each task is private or shared.
  • Tugs and share prompts: short messages you send to your partner about shared tasks or empty states.
  • Screen time check-ins: your self-reported daily rating (Light, Normal, or A lot). We do not collect OS screen-time data, app usage minutes, or device monitoring.
  • App opens: dates you open the app while paired, used only for cord streaks.
  • Notification preferences: toggles and quiet-hour settings you configure.
  • Pairing codes: temporary 6-character codes used to connect with one partner.

What we do not collect

  • Location data
  • Contacts or address book
  • Photos, camera, or microphone data
  • Advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking
  • Device screen-time APIs or per-app usage statistics
  • Payment information (Tether is free in v1)

How partner sharing works

Tasks are private by default. Your partner can only see tasks you explicitly mark as shared. When paired, your partner can view your shared task title, description, due date, and completion status. They cannot edit or complete your tasks. If you unpair or delete your account, shared visibility ends immediately and your shared tasks revert to private on your side before removal.

How we use your information

  • Provide sign-in, sync, pairing, tasks, tugs, and streak features
  • Deliver notifications you opt into
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, and fix bugs
  • Respond to support requests

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data for advertising.

Third-party services

  • Supabase hosts authentication, database, and realtime sync.
  • Google Sign In authenticates your account.
  • Apple Push Notification service (APNs) may deliver notifications if you enable them.
  • Expo Application Services (EAS) builds and distributes the app.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms and privacy policies. We only share what is needed to operate the service.

Data retention

  • Pairing codes expire 24 hours after creation.
  • Completed task history is kept for a short rolling window for in-app context.
  • Account data is kept until you delete your account or we remove it after a prolonged period of inactivity.

Your rights and choices

You can:

  • Update your profile in the app at any time
  • Unpair from your partner in Settings
  • Turn notification categories on or off in Settings
  • Sign out on any device
  • Delete your account in Settings. Deletion is permanent, removes your data from our servers, and ends any active pairing.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access, correct, export, or delete personal information. Email mrkrphl0705@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

Tether is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use industry-standard safeguards including encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS), authenticated database access, and row-level security so partners only see data you choose to share. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

International transfers

Your information may be processed in countries where our service providers operate. We take steps to ensure appropriate safeguards when data is transferred internationally.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new effective date on this page. Continued use of Tether after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email mrkrphl0705@gmail.com or visit our support page.
