Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 29, 2026

Twodo ("we", "us", or "our") is a to-do list for two people. Your day is private by default. You share a task only when you both need it. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Who we are

Twodo is operated by the developer of the Twodo iOS app. For privacy questions or account requests, contact us at mrkrphl0705@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you create an account and use Twodo, we process:

  • Account information: email address and name (or relay email) from Sign in with Apple and/or Google Sign-In.
  • Profile information: display name, partner color, timezone, and onboarding state.
  • Tasks: titles, descriptions, due dates, recurrence settings, completion status, and whether each task is private or shared.
  • Asks: requests you send so the other person can add a task only if they want to.
  • Push tokens and notification preferences: device push tokens (if you allow notifications) plus toggles and quiet-hour settings you configure.
  • Pairing codes: temporary 6-character codes used to connect with one person.

What we do not collect

  • Location data
  • Contacts or address book
  • Photos, camera, or microphone data
  • Advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking
  • Device screen-time APIs or per-app usage statistics
  • Payment information

How sharing works

Tasks are private by default. The other person can only see tasks you explicitly share. When paired, they can view a shared task's title, description, due date, and completion status. They cannot edit or complete your tasks. If you unpair or delete your account, shared visibility ends immediately and your shared tasks revert to private on your side before removal.

How we use your information

  • Provide sign-in, sync, pairing, tasks, and asks
  • Deliver notifications you opt into, including quiet hours
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, and fix bugs
  • Respond to support requests

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data for advertising.

Third-party services

  • InstantDB (Instant) hosts authentication, realtime sync, and app data storage.
  • Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In authenticate your account.
  • Apple Push Notification service (APNs) may deliver notifications if you enable them (via Expo push).
  • Cloudflare Workers may help deliver partner notifications you opt into.
  • Expo Application Services (EAS) builds and distributes the app.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms and privacy policies. We only share what is needed to operate the service. We do not sell your personal information.

Data retention

  • Pairing codes expire 24 hours after creation.
  • Completed task history is kept for a short rolling window for in-app context.
  • Account data is kept until you delete your account or we remove it after a prolonged period of inactivity.

Your rights and choices

You can:

  • Update your profile in the app at any time
  • Unpair at any time
  • Turn notification categories on or off in Me, including quiet hours
  • Sign out on any device
  • Delete your account in Me > Account > Delete account. Deletion is permanent, removes your data from our servers, and ends any active pairing.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access, correct, export, or delete personal information. Email mrkrphl0705@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

Twodo is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use industry-standard safeguards including encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS), authenticated access to our backend, and permission rules so the other person only sees data you choose to share. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

International transfers

Your information may be processed in countries where our service providers operate. We take steps to ensure appropriate safeguards when data is transferred internationally.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new effective date on this page. Continued use of Twodo after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email mrkrphl0705@gmail.com or visit our support page.
